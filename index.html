User Guide

Everything you need to know about using CAPS

Getting Started

  • Download and install CAPS.apk
  • Login with your Matric Number and Password
  • View your CGPA on the dashboard
  • Toggle Dark/Light theme using the theme button

Results & Grades

  • Click "Results" to view all semester results
  • Filter by Level (100L-400L) and Semester
  • Generate PDF reports of your results
  • View grade distribution in "Grades" section

Student Search

  • Search by Faculty or Department
  • Search by State or LGA of origin
  • Filter by Gender (Male/Female)
  • Comprehensive keyword search

Contacts & Staff

  • Find student contact information
  • Direct WhatsApp messaging
  • View Staff Directory (VC, Deans, HODs)
  • Send emails directly from the app

Data Extraction

  • Extract valid student IDs by department
  • Select year of entry and number range
  • Generate professional PDF reports
  • Share extraction results
