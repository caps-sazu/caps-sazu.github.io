## 🚀 Overview

CAPS (Comprehensive Academic Portal System) is a feature-rich mobile application designed specifically for students of **Bauchi State University, Gadau (BASUG)** now **Sa'adu Zungur University (SAZU)**. It provides seamless, offline-capable access to academic information, results, and student services directly from your Android device.

## 📖 Complete User Guide

### 1. Getting Started
- **Download** the APK from the button above or the [website](https://caps-sazu.github.io).
- **Install** it on your Android device (allow installation from unknown sources if prompted).
- **Login** with your university Matric Number and Password.
- **Explore** the dashboard – your CGPA, level, and quick actions are waiting.

### 2. Results & Grades
- Tap **Results** to see all semesters you’ve completed.
- Use the **Level** (100L–400L) and **Semester** (First/Second) filters to focus on any term.
- Hit **Print** to generate a polished PDF report – perfect for printing or sharing.
- In the **Grades** section, watch your CGPA progression on an interactive chart and see grade distribution (A–F) across all courses.

### 3. Student Search
- Choose a search type: **School**, **Location**, **Gender**, or **Comprehensive**.
- **School Search** – filter by faculty or department.
- **Location Search** – pick a state and LGA.
- **Gender Search** – male / female.
- **Comprehensive** – type any keyword (name, ID, department) and select which fields to search.
- The **multi‑threaded engine** scans thousands of records in seconds.

### 4. Contacts & Staff Directory
- **Contacts** – enter any matric number to pull up the student’s phone, email, and address, then call, WhatsApp, or email them.
- **Staffs** – browse the full university hierarchy. Tap any staff card to expand and contact them directly.

### 5. Data Extraction (for authorized users)
- **Extract** allows you to generate a PDF list of all valid student IDs for a given department and year.
- Choose the **department**, **year of entry**, and the **number of digits** in the matric number (4 or 5).
- The app automatically sets the start/end numbers. Speed is fixed for optimal performance.
- Once finished, you can open or share the PDF.

### 6. Need Help?
